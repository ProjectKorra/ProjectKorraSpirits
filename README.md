# ProjectKorraSpirits [![GitHub Pre-Release](https://img.shields.io/github/release-pre/ProjectKorra/ProjectKorraSpirits.svg)](https://github.com/ProjectKorra/ProjectKorraSpirits/releases)

## Overview

ProjectKorraSpirits is a plugin and the official Spirits extension of the 
Minecraft Avatar: The Last Airbender plugin, [ProjectKorra](https://github.com/ProjectKorra/ProjectKorra), 
bringing the mechanics of spirits from the animated series into the game.

## Planned Concepts

### Spirits

Spirits are entities that are naturally found in the Spirit World. Neutral, light, and dark
spirits are found within the Spirit World. Hostility is determined by the spirit's
alignment. Spirits can be summoned by players who have chosen to be a spirit.

Moreover, Spirits and players/benders can engage in symbiosis, where the spirit augments
the player's abilities (both Wan and Yun have displayed an aspect of combining the
essence of a spirit with their own physical bodies). 
This is a unique mechanic that allows players to gain new powers
and abilities by forming a bond with a spirit. The symbiosis can be temporary or permanent,
depending on the player's choice and the spirit's nature.

### Spirit World

The Spirit World is a separate 'dimension' or world inhabited by spirits with special
world generation.

In the Spirit World, many phenomena occur as "world events" such as **dry sun**, **heavy rain**,
**periods of darkness**, etc. Additionally, the environment of the Spirit World is 
interactive, with: slowing plants, inhabitable regions, enhanced entities (spirits), and
other features that make entering and exploring the Spirit World a unique experience!

### Spirit Items

Spirit items are special items that can be crafted by players upon the mining of a
special ore in the Spirit World.* (*tentative*)

These items can be used by any player or bender, regardless of their bending type.
